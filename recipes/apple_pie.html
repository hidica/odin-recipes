Apple Pie

Ingredients:

  • 6 cups sliced apples
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons butter, cut into small pieces

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a large bowl, mix together the sliced apples, sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt.
  3. Pour the apple mixture into a 9-inch pie dish.
  4. Cut the butter into small pieces and distribute them over the apple mixture.
  5. Bake for 15 minutes.
